Indic Letters

Consonant sounds of 15 Indic languages, laid out by where your mouth makes them. Pick your home language, then move the slider to compare it with the others, nearest-sounding first. Press a letter to hear both languages and see its details. Press a word to hear it in context.

Home language

Prototype. Cell states come from PHOIBLE (1 to 5 descriptions per language). Bishnupriya has no PHOIBLE or Wiktionary data, so its cells come from espeak-ng alone. Letter sounds are espeak-ng; a ! marks letters where espeak-ng disagrees with PHOIBLE, and pressing those plays the example word instead. Example words and their IPA come from Wiktionary (via kaikki.org); words marked ● are real speakers from Wikimedia Commons and Lingua Libre. Credits and licenses · Source
